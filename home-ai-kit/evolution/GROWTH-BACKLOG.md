## Sequencing and acceptance

Complete P0 review/preservation/evidence foundations before expanding execution. P1 items may proceed independently only where prerequisites and scope are satisfied; device suitability cannot be certified by software tests. P2/P3 are bounded follow-ups, not installation requests. Prefer existing local capabilities; inventory and measure before adding anything.

Every promoted capability needs: observed installed interface/version, ownership/scope, concrete reviewed output, actual check results tied to source hashes, documented failure/rollback, and explicit separation of staging, execution, health and functional readiness. Missing capability remains unavailable or a next-step item; no simulated result stands in for a host/compiler/browser/device run. Paid/hosted capacity is never inferred from subscription names or previous successful jobs.

For this assigned step, required checks are empty and only the six assigned output files may change. They become ready for human review once saved and reread unchanged; that is not deployment or functional certification. PowerShell/host checks are future gates, not work claimed completed here.
